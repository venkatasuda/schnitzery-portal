Schnitzery Portal

One simple place to run the day.

Today the essentials — who's working, who's off, which stock is low, whose documents are about to expire — are spread across memory, paper and chat messages. Schnitzery Portal brings all of it into one easy app that everyone opens on their phone, with each person seeing only what matters to their job.

Why it helps the business

The outcomes that matter, in plain terms.

Saves time every day. Staff clock in from their phone, and rotas, availability and time-off all live in one place instead of paper and WhatsApp.

Fewer payroll mistakes. Hours are recorded and totalled automatically, ready for payroll in one click — no more adding up timesheets by hand.

Always audit-ready. Fridge and freezer checks and food waste are logged and kept for you, and staff documents like visas and work permits are flagged before they expire.

Keeps costs in view. See staff cost against sales, and food cost, as the month unfolds — not weeks later when it's too late to act.

The whole business at a glance. Owners get one overview across every branch, and can look into any single location whenever they want the detail.

Comfortable for everyone. Works in both English and German, on any phone, with nothing to install.

What each person gets

Everyone sees a view built for their job — each line is what it does and why it matters.

Employees

The everyday view for the kitchen and floor team.

  • Clock in / out from their phone — hours are captured accurately and can't be faked, so pay is right.
  • See shifts and weekly schedule — everyone knows when they're on, so fewer missed shifts.
  • Set availability, request time-off or a shift swap — requests reach the manager without the WhatsApp back-and-forth.
  • See their own hours for the month — staff can check their pay looks right, which cuts disputes.
  • Personal documents with expiry reminders — visas and work permits never lapse unnoticed, removing a legal risk.
  • Report a problem or accident with a photo — incidents are logged with evidence for safety and insurance.
  • Log fridge/freezer temperatures and waste — the food-safety records the law needs, in seconds.
  • Read team announcements — one place for updates so nothing important gets lost.

Managers

Everything the team has, plus the tools to run a branch day-to-day.

  • Live view of who's working, late, or on break — spot gaps and lateness the moment they happen.
  • Approve time-off and shift swaps in a tap — decisions in seconds, and staff are notified instantly.
  • Build the weekly rota fast, with flexible shift times — set exact hours per shift and spot no-shows; less admin, fewer uncovered shifts.
  • Add and manage staff, check their documents — onboarding and compliance in one place.
  • Track stock, orders and transfers between branches — avoid both running out and over-ordering.
  • Enter daily sales, see labour cost vs sales instantly — control the biggest cost while the month is still live.
  • One-click monthly pay summary — payroll prepared without manual timesheet maths.
  • Branch performance with working-time checks — stay compliant on breaks, rest and overtime.

Branch owners

Everything a manager has for the branch they own — plus its performance insights, so they see the health of their location at a glance.

Owners & Head office

The whole business in one place.

  • One overview across every branch, drill into any — spot the branch that needs attention, then see the detail.
  • Compare performance, cost and staffing month by month — decisions on data, not guesswork.
  • Document compliance across all staff — audit-ready across the whole company.
  • Step into any branch's own tools whenever needed — the detail is always one tap away.

Safe sign-in and access

Security that protects staff data and the business — mostly invisible, always on.

  • Role-based access — each person sees only what their job needs; enforced in the database, so one branch can never see another's data.
  • Forced password change on first login — the shared onboarding password is replaced immediately, so no shared secret lingers.
  • Automatic lockout after repeated wrong tries — stops password guessing; a manager can unlock in a tap.
  • Manager password reset — no company email needed; when someone forgets, a manager issues a one-time code on the spot.
  • Automatic employee IDs per branch — every hire gets a clean branch code (e.g. STG-001), with no duplicates as the business grows across cities.
  • Instant notifications — approvals, low stock and announcements reach the right phone straight away.
