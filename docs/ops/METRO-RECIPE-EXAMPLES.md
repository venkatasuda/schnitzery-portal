## Acceptance rules

- Compatible grams/kilograms and millilitres/litres convert exactly.
- All branches share the supplied reference prices; recipe quantities may vary.
- Missing prices remain unknown, never zero; incomplete recipes withhold contribution and cost percentages.
- No selling prices were supplied, so no menu margin can be inferred.
- Examples must not create live catalogue items, purchase receipts or stock deductions automatically.
